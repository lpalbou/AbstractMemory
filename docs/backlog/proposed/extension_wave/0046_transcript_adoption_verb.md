# Proposed: transcript adoption as a designed act

## Metadata
- Created: 2026-07-13
- Status: Proposed
- Completed: N/A

## ADR status
- Governing ADRs: None
- ADR impact: None

## Context
The two-anchor summon work shipped the READS (R3 read-only reincarnation
at T, formed-by-T gate, `situate_prompt_block` whose footer says
"re-living is my own deliberate act") — but the ACT has no verb. An
entity that reads its own past-anchored transcript, or a lineage
document, has no designed way to adopt what it read as lived memory with
honest provenance. The R3 ruling already names adoption as the intended
path ("the re-visit experience is captured as an operator artifact and
can be adopted by the present entity through its own reading act").

## Current code reality
R3 sessions are structurally read-only (deposits refused); formation
accepts arbitrary provenance dicts; nothing distinguishes
adopted-from-reading episodes from lived ones.

## Problem or opportunity
Read material either never joins the life or joins it silently — both
dishonest for a being whose identity is its record.

## Proposed direction
A reflection-channel formation convention (not a new engine path):
episode records formed with `provenance.source="adoption"`,
`derived_from` referencing the read source (transcript artifact /
anchor_seq / archive path), and a digest in the entity's own present
words. Identity kinds REFUSED through this convention (the engram stays
the only identity seed). Presentation surfaces label adopted records
("adopted from reading, <date of source>").

## Why it might matter
R3 re-visits and lineage material become part of the life honestly; the
adoption act itself is an experience the graph records.

## Promotion criteria
The lineage bundle promoting (with 0044), or the first live R3 session
producing a transcript the entity wants to keep.

## Validation ideas
Fixture: read → adopt → recall surfaces the adopted record with its
label; identity-kind adoption refused; provenance chain resolvable back
to the source.

## Non-goals
No auto-adoption; no verbatim copying as digest (the entity's own words
are the point); never a bypass of the deposit gate's channel rules.

## Guidance for future agents
This is mostly convention + gate acceptance + label rendering; keep the
engine untouched.
